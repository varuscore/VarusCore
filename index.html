The problem

Patients message at all hours.
Your front desk can't.

Enquiries land after closing, during lunch, in the middle of a busy clinic. When no one replies fast, the patient books with whoever does.

After-hours enquiries go cold

A large share of WhatsApp enquiries arrive outside clinic hours. By the time someone replies the next morning, the patient has often moved on.

Four languages, one front desk

Booking back-and-forth

Every enquiry becomes a chain of messages to find a slot. Multiply that across a day and the front desk is buried in admin instead of patients.
