Patient reactivation is reaching out to patients who haven't visited in 9–18+ months to bring them back. Most haven't switched clinics — they drifted away. A well-timed, personalised message via WhatsApp is often all it takes. We handle the outreach, you handle the bookings when patients reply.
From campaign data and industry benchmarks for Singapore dental and medical clinics, a properly run reactivation typically produces a 4–10% rebooking rate on a 9–18 month dormant list. For a clinic with 300 dormant patients, that's 12–30 rebookings. This depends on how clean your data is, how long patients have been dormant, and how relevant your clinic is to them still. We'll give you a realistic estimate on the discovery call.
